# Glow Strikers — Game Design Document (running spec)

**Status:** shipped on the StarHermit engine 1.17.0 (host API 1.19, `engine.minor=19`); this document describes the game as it runs today.
**Version:** rules v1, content v1, ruleset build `1.0.0` (`js/session.js` `RULESET_BUILD`).

## 1. Overview

**Pitch:** a luminous tabletop striking sport — defend the glowing goal at your end and drive a low-friction puck through the far one with a circular mallet, on a neon table whose rails are live.

## 2. Vision and design pillars

1. **The rails are the second player.** Bank shots off the luminous rails (restitution 0.92, no energy gain) are the intended way past a defender; lesson 5 teaches it and the AI's defensive lag rewards it. Rules in: symmetric obstacles, wide 44-unit goal mouths, a lively mallet coefficient (1.06). Rules out: curved shots, spin, power-ups, anything that makes the puck's path unreadable from the geometry.
2. **One fixed tick, one truth.** The whole game — solo, undo, replay verification and the hosted server script — runs the same `rules.step` at 60 Hz with a seeded RNG that only decides serve angles. Rules in: quantised commands, per-tick hashes, a server that never trusts client physics. Rules out: client-side prediction, variable time steps, cosmetic randomness touching outcomes.
3. **Your half is yours.** The mallet can never cross the centre line, and every mode changes what you can do inside your half (clock, travel budget, bumpers) rather than the table itself. Rules in: budgets, shutouts, mastery targets. Rules out: asymmetric tables, opponent-side interaction.
4. **Glow is information.** Every emissive surface means something: warm pink is you, cool cyan is the opponent, goal strips pulse while play is live, the drag marker is grounded (never bloom-only), rings breathe to show ownership. Rules in: five themes that recolour but never restate. Rules out: decorative glow on the play surface without a gameplay referent, effects that could hide the puck; ambient arena glow (floor grid, motes, pillar caps, underglow) stays off the table and dimmer than gameplay emissives.
5. **Nothing is audio-only, nothing is visual-only.** Every cue has a caption, every state change is announced to screen readers, and a board-state narration panel describes the arena in words. Rules in: real UI widgets (focusable, labelled) for every action, play scripts that drive the real UI. Rules out: hidden gestures, unlabelled drawn controls.

## 3. Player experience

**Target player:** someone who wants a two-minute reflex duel with just enough system depth (par, stars, budgets, seeds) to return daily. Mobile thumb play and desktop mouse play are equal citizens.

**First 60 seconds.** The title screen offers a primary **Play** button (Quick Match, first-to-5 vs a Club-strength AI) two taps from the arena; the setup card states the win condition, expected duration, players and ranked status before commitment. In play, the HUD objective ("First to 5") sits top-left, a 3-second countdown ticks with a banner, and the serve banner reads "GO". Journey stage 1 carries `tutorialFlags: ['move','strike']`, and **Learn** is a six-lesson track where each lesson requires the action (move 40 units → strike → survive 6 s → score → bank a rail → short match). Pressing **H** at any point captions a contextual hint derived from `rules.legalActions` and the puck's half. **How to Play** is reachable from the title and from pause.

**Session shape.** Countdown (3 s) → rally → goal (1.5 s pause, conceder receives) → … → results with a seven-row breakdown (goals, shots, saves, steals, invalid actions, rail bounces, fastest strike) plus duration and terminal reason. Journey chains stages with a "Next: …" button; Retry restarts the same seed.

**The emotional beat** is the deep save that turns into a counter-strike: the puck heading at your goal, the block from inside your third (the rules count it as a save and the game now cues it), and the rebound already travelling toward the far goal.

## 4. Core loop and rules contract (`js/rules.js`)

**Table and entities.** Table 100 × 200 units (`TABLE_W`, `TABLE_H`); player 0 defends y = 0 and attacks toward y = 200, player 1 mirrors. Mallet radius 7, puck radius 4, goal mouth 44 units centred on each end. Obstacles are static circles from `content.js` layouts. Fixed tick `DT = 1/60` s.

**Phases** (`PHASE`): `countdown` (180 ticks) → `active` → `goalPause` (90 ticks) → `active` … → `terminal`.

**Legal actions** (`legalActions(state, player)`): exactly one action type, `move`, with the player's clamped bounds from `malletBounds` (x ∈ [7, 93]; y ∈ [7, 93] for player 0, [107, 193] for player 1). During `active` a player whose move budget is ≤ 0 has no legal actions (reason `move-budget-exhausted`); after `terminal` nobody has (reason `match-over`).

**Commands** (`applyCommand`): `move {x,y}` sets the mallet target, clamped into bounds; `forfeit` ends the match for the opponent. Rejections carry a reason (`malformed-command`, `unknown-player`, `match-over`, `bad-target`, `move-budget-exhausted`, `unknown-command-type`) and never throw; only the budget rejection increments `stats[p].invalid`. `Session.move` quantises targets to 0.25 units, drops unchanged targets and duplicate ids, and logs accepted commands to the replay envelope.

**Resolution order per active tick** (`stepActive`):
1. Each mallet (0 then 1) moves toward its target at most `malletSpeeds[i] × DT` (default 150 u/s); with a move budget the step is capped by the remaining budget and a `budget-exhausted` event fires when it hits zero. Mallet velocity is derived from the displacement.
2. Puck velocity × `PUCK_FRICTION` (0.9985 per tick), clamped to `MAX_PUCK_SPEED` 300 u/s, then integrated. The last toucher's `maxPuckSpeed` is updated.
3. Mallet–puck collision, player 0 first: depenetrate to radius 11, reflect the relative normal velocity with coefficient `1 + MALLET_REST` (2.06), clamp. Stats: a strike sending the puck toward the opponent counts a `shot`; if the puck was incoming and the intercept was inside the striker's third it also counts a `save`; a strike that is not toward the opponent but takes the puck from the other player counts a `steal`. Emits `strike {player, speed}`.
4. Obstacles: depenetrate and mirror the normal velocity (restitution 1). Emits `obstacle`.
5. Side rails: reflect with `WALL_REST` 0.92, emits `wall`, credits a `wallBounce` to the last toucher.
6. End rails and goals: if |x − 50| < 22 and the puck is leaving the table through an end, `goal(state, scorer)`; otherwise reflect like a rail.
7. Move-limit check: both budgets ≤ 0 and post-collision puck speed < 5 u/s ends the match (`move-limit`).
Then `checkTerminal` handles time limits.

**Goals and serves** (`goal`, `serve`): a goal increments the scorer's score and `goals` stat, recentres the puck, and either ends the match (target reached, or any goal in overtime) or schedules a `goalPause` after which the **conceder receives** the serve. The serve speed is 70 u/s at a seeded angle within ±30° of the receiver's direction; the opening serve direction is a seeded coin flip.

**Terminal states** (`TERMINAL`): `target-score` (first to `targetScore`, default 5), `time-limit` (clock expired with a leader, or tied with overtime disabled → `winner = -1`, a draw), `overtime-goal` (tied at the clock with overtime enabled → golden goal), `forfeit` (explicit command; also issued by the client when a Perfect Wall challenge concedes, and by the server when a seat is abandoned), `move-limit` (both budgets spent, puck at rest; tie → draw).

**Scoring formulas** (`native/main.ts`):
- Journey stars (`computeStars`): 1 for the win, +1 if margin ≥ `par.winBy` (2; 3 on mastery stages), +1 if there is no clock or elapsed ≤ `par.underSeconds` (80 % of the limit; 120 s when unclocked). *Example:* stage 12 (limit 126 s, `winBy` 2) won 5–2 in 88 s → 1 + 1 (margin 3) + 1 (88 ≤ 101) = 3 stars.
- Daily and challenge board score: `yourGoals × 10 − theirGoals`. *Example:* Daily won 5–3 → 47; lost 2–5 → 15 (losses are still posted for the daily).
- Journey mastery board score: `stars × 1000 − elapsedSeconds`. *Example:* 3 stars in 84 s → 2916.
Local boards (`js/progress.js` `submitResult`) reject wrong rulesets or content versions, non-integer or out-of-range scores (journey −1800…3000, others −99…99) and implausible durations (< 1 s or > 30 min), then sort by score desc, duration asc, keeping 20.

**RNG and seeding** (`js/rng.js`): `mulberry32` seeded per stream (`RngStream(seed, stream)` mixes the stream tag with the golden ratio). Rules use stream 0 and only draw for the opening serve side and each serve angle; `rngCursor` records draws so `deserialize` rebuilds the identical stream. The AI uses `seed ^ 0xA11CE` on stream 7; audio pitch variants use a fixed cosmetic stream 3. Journey seeds are `0xC0FFEE + index × 7919`; challenges carry fixed seeds; the daily seed is `(YYYYMMDD × 2654435761) >>> 0`; Quick Match and Practice draw a random 31-bit seed.

**Undo and hints.** In Practice and Learn only, `Session` snapshots every 60 ticks (keeps 40) and **Z** / the HUD Undo button restores the latest (`doUndo`). **H** captions a hint (`giveHint`). Neither exists in ranked modes or Hosted Play.

**Determinism and replay.** `Session` records commands with ticks, a hash every 60 ticks, and the final hash; `Session.verifyReplay` re-simulates from seed and commands and fails on the first mismatching hash. `rules.hash` is FNV-1a over a key-sorted serialization.

## 5. Modes and progression

| Mode (title button) | Content | Opponent | Clock / rules | Ranked | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Play (Quick Match) | random seed, no obstacles | skill 0.50 | first to 5, no clock, overtime on | no | runs as `practice` mode (undo available) |
| Daily Challenge | `dailyConfig(UTC day)` | 0.55–0.84 by seed | first to 5, 120 s, golden goal | yes | layout = seed mod 6 of {none, pillars, gates, diamonds, cross, hive}, theme = seed mod 5; wins add the day to `dailyDays` |
| Journey | 40 authored stages | 0.20 → 0.95 | see below | mastery stages only | stage n unlocks when n−1 is completed; stars 1–3 |
| Challenges | 6 fixed cards | 0.30–0.75 | per card | yes | Blitz Clock (3–0 in 60 s, no overtime), Tight Ledger (1500-unit budget, first to 2), Pinball Hive, Perfect Wall (shutout: any concession forfeits), Long Night (first to 9, skill 0.75), Needle Gates (gates, 90 s) |
| Learn | 6 lessons | none (lesson 6: skill 0.25) | goal-driven | no | each lesson requires the action; completing all six sets `tutorialDone` and unlocks Quick Learner |
| Practice | random seed | Relaxed 0.25 / Club 0.50 / Pro 0.75 / Legend 0.92 | first to 5 | no | Restart and Undo available |
| Hosted Play | StarHermit realtime room: create, quick join, join by code, friend invites | a second human, or the room's AI seat at 0.55 | first to 5, 180 s, golden goal | refereed and recorded by `server.js` (room result) | signed in only; room chat |

**Journey curve** (`buildJourney`): stages sit in five bands of eight. AI skill = min(0.95, 0.18 + 0.02·i) (+0.08 on mastery). Layouts progress none → pillars → gates → diamonds → cross → hive by band, stepping early in the last two stages of each band. Band 2+ adds a clock, max(75, 150 − 2·i) seconds. Band 3+ adds a player-only travel budget on every third stage, max(2600, 5200 − 60·i) units. Mastery stages (every 8th) play to 7 and post to the journey board. Each band uses the next of the five themes.

**Achievements** (`ACHIEVEMENTS`, idempotent, kept in the save document (device and cloud slot)): First Victory, Quick Learner (all lessons), Heating Up (3-win streak), Mastery Proven, Century of Light (100 goals), Daily Regular (3 daily days).

**Difficulty knobs the AI exposes** (`createAI`): decision cadence `max(1, round(12 − 10·skill))` ticks, aim error `(1 − skill) × 14` units re-rolled every 90 ticks, puck lead `0.2 + 0.6·skill` seconds. Behaviours: intercept from behind the puck when it is in its half, guard the goal line tracking puck x with lag when the puck is incoming, otherwise drift home shading the lane.

## 6. Controls and interaction

Actions (`starhermit.txt`; players rebind keys and pads in the runtime's Options, F1):

| Action | Keys | Gamepad | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| `up` / `down` / `left` / `right` | Arrows | left stick / d-pad | move the target 140 u/s (a stick proportionally); target ring shown |
| `pause` | Escape | Start | pause / resume; in Hosted Play opens the Match Menu; also backs out of screens |
| `undo` | Z | West | undo (Practice, Learn) |
| `camera` | C | right-stick press | recentre the camera (0.9 s transition) |
| `hint` | H | North | caption + announcement |
| `board` | B | Select | toggle the board-state narration |

| Input | Desktop | Phone | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pointer on the table | mouse movement moves the mallet (no press) unless **Hold to aim** is on; a press-drag always does | a finger dragging on the table (a press that starts off the HUD's buttons) | target ring; mallet follows at ≤ 150 u/s |
| HUD buttons | Pause (II), Undo (Practice/Learn), Board state | same | as the actions |

Input is taken only while a match is live and no screen is open (`inputAllowed`); a press is decided after the frame's UI is built, so presses on HUD buttons never move the mallet. In a hosted match from seat 1 the camera sits at the far end and the arrows are mirrored, so your mallet is always the near one. Haptics (`vibrate`, the game's Haptics setting): 8 ms on a strike, 60 ms on a goal. Backgrounding the window (`onSuspend`) pauses a solo match.

## 7. Screens and UI flow

`app.screen` (`native/main.ts`): `title → {journey | challenges | learn | setup | settings | help | achievements | leaderboard | lobby | expired}`; `null` while playing (the HUD), `pause` over a live match, `results` after it. Each screen is one panel centred over the arena that scrolls inside when taller than the window, its footer buttons pinned below; the first primary control takes the focus when it opens.

Layouts by the window's logical size (`layoutNow`): **wide** (≥ 1024 px) — the title panel has a left rail (stages cleared, wins · losses, best streak) and a right rail (Achievements, Leaderboards, Settings, How to Play, Sign in / Invite a friend); **compact** — no rails, the same buttons in a wrapping More row under the menu; **portrait phone** (≤ 700 px wide, taller than wide) — 16 px panel padding, smaller objective pill, key art 110 px; **short landscape** (≤ 500 px tall) — 14 px padding, key art 70 px. Everything respects the safe area and the runtime's interface size.

HUD: objective pill (top-left, wraps), score pills and clock (top-centre; yours pink, theirs cyan; `OT` in overtime), Pause (top-right); the banner (countdown, GO, GOAL!, Conceded, OVERTIME, lesson banners; "Waiting for your opponent…" in a hosted match until both players are connected); the travel budget pill (bottom-left), captions (bottom-centre), Undo and Board state (bottom-right; mirrored with **Left-handed layout**); the board-state panel above them; toasts above the bottom edge.

Screens: **Title** (key art, logo, tagline, account line, Play / Daily Challenge / Journey / Challenges / Learn / Practice / Hosted Play with their second lines); **Journey** (40 rows: number, name, mechanics, MASTERY tag or stars, locked rows disabled); **Challenges** and **Learn** (rows with ✓ when done); **Setup** (title, description, four cells — win condition, expected duration, players, ranked — the Practice difficulty select, Start / Back); **Pause** (Paused or Match Menu: Resume / Back to Match, Settings, How to Play, Restart (solo), Leave Match); **Results** (illustration, headline, context, stars, the seven-row breakdown table, duration and reason, achievements unlocked, the leaderboard line, Retry / Next / Menu); **How to Play** (six cards with the current key bindings); **Settings** (captions, colour palette, left-handed layout, hold to aim, timing assistance, haptics, Replay lessons, and Open Options for the runtime's sound, graphics, language, controls and accessibility); **Achievements**; **Leaderboards** (the platform board, today's daily and journey mastery personal bests); **Hosted Play** (section 12); **Session expired / Updated terms** (Play on this device).

Must never be cut off: the objective and both score pills, the pause button, the results table, the Start button on setup cards, and the room code in the lobby.

## 8. Art direction

**Palette** (`tools/ui-style.css` tokens → `assets/ui-style.json`): background `#070a18`, panel `rgba(13,18,40,.92)` / solid `#0d1228`, line `#2a3a66`, text `#e8ecff`, dim `#9aa6d0`, accent cyan `#38e6ff`, warm pink `#ff5c8a`, gold `#ffd166`, danger `#ff6b6b`, ok `#51ff9e`. CVD palettes swap the accent pair (deuteranopia `#4dd7ff`/`#ffb84d`, protanopia `#59d8ff`/`#ffd24d`, tritanopia `#4dffd2`/`#ff5c5c`); high contrast uses pure white text on near-black panels with `#8fa4ff` lines.

**Arena themes** (`content.js` `THEMES`, presentation only): Neon Dusk (rail `#38e6ff`, you `#ff5c8a`, accent `#ffd166`), Solar Foundry (`#ffb02e` / `#ff4545` / `#7ef0c1`), Verdant Grid (`#51ff9e` / `#ffe066` / `#ff8fb3`), Violet Abyss (`#b06bff` / `#5cf2ff` / `#ffd166`), Glacier Line (`#9adcff` / `#ff9e5c` / `#b4ff6b`). Each defines bg, floor, table, line, rail, goal colours, puck, both mallets, accent and a bloom strength (0.7–1.0).

**Shape language.** Everything is a cylinder, torus or slab: a 112 × 212 slab table, 2.4-unit emissive rail boxes with goal gaps, a flat puck disc, mallets as a tapered cylinder + emissive torus ring + dark handle, bumpers as cylinders with an accent rim, and a ring of 16 glowing-capped pillars at radius 200 (detailed tiers). Rails are dark metal housings with a luminous core strip on top; the puck carries a thin rail-coloured emissive rim; mallet handles end in a lit cap. The playing surface is a procedural `starhermit:canvas` texture (the level's surface width × twice it on detailed tiers, 256 × 512 plain): base tone and sheen, and on detailed tiers fine grain (the browser build's LCG over a 128-px tile, drawn as a repeating pattern) plus an air-hole grid; its markings (centre line and circle, centre spot, two goal boxes) also feed a faint emissive map with a soft halo so they read on every tier. On the detailed tier the floor carries a rail-coloured emissive grid, and every tier gets a soft additive underglow of the rail colour spilling from beneath the table.

**Hero of the screen:** the puck and its rail-coloured additive trail (26 points), framed from behind your own goal (camera fov 46, height 165, 118 units back, look-ahead 18; portrait pulls back ×1.55 and up ×1.45).

**Typography:** the runtime's UI font; the title in 900-weight letter-spaced capitals (a solid blue-grey: text gradients do not exist in the engine's UI); text size follows the runtime's Accessibility options.

**Motion.** Camera transitions are cubic-eased over 0.9 s; goal shake 0.5, terminal shake 0.8, decaying fast; particle bursts (strike 10, rail 5, goal 60, terminal 120) with gravity and a floor bounce, capped at 300 (Particles: low) or 2000 (high). Goal strips pulse while play is live; your ring breathes. **Reduced motion** (the runtime's Accessibility option) removes shake, makes camera transitions instant, drops the banner glow, freezes the ambient arena motion and the menu orbit; particles keep their timing but tiers already bound counts.

**Graphics.** ACES filmic tone mapping (exposure 1); a hemisphere fill, a key directional light with a soft view-based shadow (`shadow.distance` 400, radius 2) and a cool point fill, intensities three.js r160's ÷ π. With reflections on, `PMREMGenerator.fromScene(new RoomEnvironment(renderer), 0.04)` is the scene environment and the pieces keep their tuned `envMapIntensity` (surface 0.08, mallets 0.25, puck 0.3, rail housings 0.4). Detailed tiers with the physical budget use clearcoated `MeshPhysicalMaterial` on the table, surface, puck, mallets, rails and bumpers; Balanced uses `MeshStandardMaterial`, Low Lambert. Burst particles are one `Points` pool with an HDR-bright core (additive); ambient light motes drift up around (never over) the table; pillar caps shimmer and the floor grid breathes (animated tiers; frozen under reduced motion). On menus and in the lobby the renderer draws an attract view: the pieces at rest and the camera orbiting slowly; starting a match eases into the play framing. Post effects are the runtime's: `renderer.ao` (GTAO radius 6, 8 or 16 samples, blend 0.7), `renderer.bloom` (strength = theme bloom × 0.75, radius 0.5, threshold 0.9: rails, rings, strips and hot highlights bloom), `renderer.grade` (display space: contrast 0.11, saturation 1.12, cool shadows / warm highlights split 0.2–0.8, vignette 0.28 from 0.38 to 0.9) and `renderer.antialias`.

**Graphics levels.** The runtime's Options › Graphics owns Auto and the ten levels, render scale, the frame-rate limit and the readout; the game reads `frame().quality` and `frame().levelIndex` (`native/quality.ts`) for the browser build's preset table, and Low/Balanced/High/Ultra keep the old look per tier:

**Visual assets the design calls for:** title key art (`assets/key-art.webp`), a victory and a defeat illustration for the results card (`assets/results-victory.webp`, `assets/results-defeat.webp`), the store cover (`coverart.png`) derived from the key art, and the icon/favicon pair. No hero 3D model: mallets and puck are procedural so every theme can recolour their emissives.

## 9. Audio direction

**Mix.** The runtime's buses (Options › Audio owns master/music/effects/interface volumes, mute and the sound limit): music → `music`, effects, ambience and cues → `effects`, UI taps → `interface`; the browser build's default levels (music 0.6, effects 0.8, ambience 0.25) are folded into each sound's gain. Audio starts with the player's first press in browsers (natively at once); clips load one at a time once the mixer runs. Ambience is a 55 Hz + 82.5 Hz sine pad breathing with a 1/14 Hz LFO, rendered once into a seamless 14 s loop (`createClip`). Music is a two-stem loop on a 300 ms beat scheduled by mixer time: a triangle bass on a four-bar `A A C G` figure every other beat, plus a sine arpeggio that fades in when intensity (puck speed ÷ 220) exceeds 0.35; every note is a clip rendered once with the browser build's envelope. Music starts with a match and stops on results or leaving.

**Clips.** Each logical event plays its authored clip (`native/audio.ts` `SAMPLES`); a strike's gain follows the puck's speed. The synthesized stand-ins the browser build played while a clip loaded are not ported (a live synthesis graph makes the web runtime drop clip loads). When **Captions** is on, meaningful cues also write to the HUD caption line.

## 10. Localization

Every player-facing string ships in nine locales: **en-US, en-GB, es-419, es-ES, de-DE, fr-FR, fr-CA, pt-BR, it-IT**, in the interface language the runtime reports (`preferences().language`: the player's choice in Options › Language, else the account's preferred language, else the device's; mapped to the nearest shipped locale, `js/i18n/locale.js`). A change applies at once (open screens are rebuilt every frame; the HUD objective re-renders).

- **Tables.** English source strings are the keys. `js/i18n/strings.js` (UI, HUD, captions, lobby) and `js/i18n/strings-content.js` (40 stage names, 6 challenges, 6 lessons, 6 achievements) hold rows of [English, es-419, de-DE, fr-FR, pt-BR, it-IT]; en-GB, es-ES and fr-CA list only their differences. The account strings (sign-in, invites, leaderboard lines, session dialogs) are `js/platform-strings.js`. "Glow Strikers", "StarHermit", room codes, player names and chat text stay as they are.
- **Applied** through `T(key, vars)` / `P(key)` (`native/i18n.ts`); `ui.setLanguage` gets the locale.
- **Expansion.** Labels wrap (`ui.paragraph` when they do not fit), buttons wrap their faces, button rows wrap.

## 11. Accessibility

- **Keyboard and gamepad:** every screen is focusable widgets (Tab/arrows, Enter/South); Escape/Start backs out; the first primary control takes the focus; gameplay actions are rebindable in the runtime's Options.
- **Screen readers:** widgets are labelled (journey rows read "Stage N: name, completed with S stars" / "locked"); the objective, score changes and hints are announced politely, banners and results assertively (`ui.announce`).
- **Captions:** the caption line mirrors audio cues (game setting, on by default).
- **Board-state narration:** Board state (button or B) shows a panel describing score, puck column/row, direction and speed, and your mallet position, refreshed twice a second.
- **Contrast and colour:** the runtime's high contrast swaps in white text and `#8fa4ff` lines; three CVD palettes (game setting) swap the accent pair; ownership is reinforced by position (you are always at the near end) and warm-vs-cool hues.
- **Motion and timing:** the runtime's reduced motion (section 8); Timing assistance runs solo matches at 85 %; Hold to aim for pointer users who cannot hover.
- **Text and interface size** are the runtime's and apply over the game's styles; targets are ≥ 44 px.

## 12. StarHermit integration

`starhermit.txt`: `name`, `description`, `owner`, `server=server.js`, `cover=cover.jpg`, `launch=index.html`, `engine.api=1`, `engine.entry=native/main.ts`, `engine.version=1.17.0`, `engine.minor=19`, `engine.capabilities=platform,storage,clipboard`, the `control.*` / `engine.gamepad.*` / `engine.group.*` lines of section 6.

- **Player:** `player()`; the title shows "Playing as <name> · cloud save synced/saving…/offline". Guests see **Sign in with StarHermit** (`requestSignIn`); signed in, **Invite a friend** copies the invite link (`clipboard.writeText`).
- **Saves** (`starhermit:storage` `saves`, schema 1): the progress document of `js/progress.js` — cleared stages, stars, totals, streaks, lessons, daily days, challenges, cosmetics, achievements and personal-best boards — on the device and in the account's cloud slot; a conflict merges both copies (`mergeSaves`: unions, the best stars, the larger counts, the earliest unlock; the current streak and cosmetics stay this device's), and an account save that replaced the device's is merged in. **Migration:** the browser build's slot `game:<slug>` (the JS SDK's zipped `save.json` holding `{ checksum, payload }`) shows as foreign; the game verifies the checksum, merges its progress, takes its game settings where this account has none (captions, palette, hold to aim, timing assistance, haptics, left-handed, tutorial flag) and replaces the slot once (`replaceForeign`). Guests' old browser localStorage progress does not carry over.
- **Settings** (`settings`, `game.<key>`, synced with the account): the game's own options above. The browser build's per-player settings keys (`settings.legacyKeys()`) are migrated where the game still has the option and removed; volumes, mute, graphics, reduced motion, high contrast, text scale and the language are the runtime's Options now and are dropped. The browser build's own key remaps are replaced by the runtime's rebinding.
- **Leaderboard read:** the Leaderboards screen lists the `high-score` board's first 20 (guests too), names from profiles.
- **Score posting:** signed in, every finished Daily match (not a draw) and every won Challenge posts its match score (your goals × 10 − conceded) through `server.js`: a practice session (`sessions.practice()`) whose game socket sends `{ type: 'result', scores: { 'high-score': n } }`; the script range-checks it (−99…99) and returns it in `scores`. The results card shows "Posting score…", then the rank, "posted" or "not posted". Quick Match, Practice, Journey, Learn and Hosted Play post nothing to the board.
- **Hosted Play** (signed in): a room is a 1v1 table (`rooms.create({ teamCount: 2, seatsPerTeam: 1, backfillAfterSeconds: 3600 })` + `open`): it waits for an opponent, who quick-joins (`rooms.quickJoin`), joins by the room's code (shown to the host) or accepts an invite (the host's friends list, `rooms.invite`; incoming invites with Accept / Decline). The host may **Start vs AI** (`rooms.start`: the platform gives the empty seat to its AI participant). An unfinished room of the player's (`rooms.mine`) is adopted when the lobby opens. When the room starts the platform binds a session to it; the client opens its game socket (`connectGame`), sends `{ type: 'sync' }` on every (re)open and its mallet target as realtime input `{ x, y }` (≤ 30 a second, when it changes), and draws the script's 30 Hz snapshots interpolated between the last two, its events (sound, effects, banners) and its result. **`server.js` referees the whole match** (section 13): nothing a client says decides the outcome; the result is stored on the room. Leave Match concedes (`{ type: 'forfeit' }`) and leaves the room. Room chat rides the room socket (`sendChat`, 10 a minute). Reconnects are the runtime's (a resync follows; a "while you were away" toast gives the score); the session ending (signed out, updated terms) shows the Session expired / Updated terms dialog; a room that is gone returns to the title with a toast.
- **Platform clock:** the Daily seed and its countdown use `serverNow()` (the device's clock until a reading arrives).
- Not used: platform achievements (the game's achievements stay in its save document), Elo, matchmaking queues, voice.

## 13. Technical architecture

- **Solo loop:** `setFixedRate(60)`; per fixed step while a match is live and no screen is open: keyboard/stick → AI move → `Session.tick` → events → lesson evaluation → HUD; with Timing assistance a step is skipped every so often (85 %). `onRender` interpolates the previous and current positions with the runtime's alpha. Lesson delays count fixed steps. Only `Session` mutates rules state.
- **Hosted referee** (`server.js`, `tickRateHz: 30`): `createSession` with `ctx.room` seats the frozen roster (team 0 = seat 0 at y = 0, team 1 = seat 1; AI seats and empty seats play `js/ai.js` at skill 0.55) and creates a first-to-5, 180 s match with golden-goal overtime seeded from `ctx.random`. It waits for both players' sockets (`ctx.presence[*].online`, at most 15 s), then each tick applies the latest realtime input per player (`ctx.inputs[i].from` → seat; the rules clamp the target into that player's half), runs `round(elapsed / 16.7 ms)` rules steps (1–6) with the AI, and broadcasts a snapshot `{ type: 'snap', k, ph, pt, p, m, s, at, tl, ot, ev }` (positions to 0.01). A player whose presence is `left` (left the room) or offline for 30 s forfeits; `{ type: 'forfeit' }` concedes. At the terminal state it broadcasts `{ type: 'over', winner, reason, scores, breakdown, finalHash }` and returns `result` `{ kind, reason, winnerSeat, winnerUserId, teamScores }`. The AI's memory and the rules state are serialized into `sessionState` every tick (`Rules.serialize`, `createAI({ saved })`), since the script keeps nothing between invocations. Other sessions are score posting (the former `score-script.js`).
- **Persistence:** section 12; nothing else is stored.
- **Content validation** (`validateContent`) runs at start (a warning) and in tests.
- **Performance:** procedural textures are made once per theme and size (at most four cached); the grain is a 128-px tile; the particle pool is fixed (1,200) with no per-frame allocation; arena rebuilds dispose what they made. The referee tick takes about 1 ms in the platform's Jint worker (slowest 10 ms of 250).

## 16. Known limitations

- Hosted Play could not be played live here (two signed-in clients on the platform): the referee is tested in node and in the platform's Jint worker, the client side follows the wiki's rooms and game-socket contract.
- Hosted matches are recorded on the room (script `result`) but post no leaderboard score or Elo, as before.
- Leaderboards: only Daily and won Challenge match scores reach the platform board (client total, range-checked by the script — the user's decision for solo modes); other personal bests are in the save document. Achievements stay in the save document (not platform achievements).
- Theme is chosen by content only; `cosmetics.theme` is fixed at Neon Dusk with no picker.
- Only budget rejections count as invalid actions; `checkTerminal` has an unreachable overtime branch (harmless, kept for replay compatibility).
- The browser build's per-category graphics overrides, adaptive resolution, frosted menu blur, telemetry queue (never uploaded) and its own Language/Sound/Report panels are gone: the runtime's Options cover graphics, sound, language and accessibility.
- **Left-handed layout** (stored but dead in the browser build) now mirrors the HUD's bottom row.
- Gamepads: the browser build read only the left stick and Start; the engine port binds every action to a pad (section 6).
- Guests' browser-localStorage progress from the old build does not migrate (only cloud saves do).
